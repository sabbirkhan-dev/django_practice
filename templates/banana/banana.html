History of banana

The Banana is one of the oldest cultivated fruits in the world. It is believed to have originated in Southeast Asia, especially in regions that are now Malaysia, Indonesia, and Papua New Guinea. 6 Short History Bananas were first domesticated around 7,000–10,000 years ago. Ancient traders spread bananas from Southeast Asia to India and later to the Middle East and Africa. Arab traders helped introduce bananas to Africa. Portuguese sailors later carried bananas to the Americas during the 15th and 16th centuries. Today bananas are grown in many tropical countries including Bangladesh, India, Brazil, and Ecuador. Interesting Facts Most modern bananas come from the Cavendish variety. Wild bananas originally contained many hard seeds. Bananas are technically classified as berries in botany.
